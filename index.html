Method, not a field system

A compact demonstration of traceable geometry handling.

A seeded generator samples two sides of a deliberately simplified surface. One bounded region is omitted before point IDs are assigned, making the coverage gap reproducible and visible. Measurements use ordinary Euclidean distance in fictional coordinate units.
